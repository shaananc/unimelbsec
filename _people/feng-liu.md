summary: "Dr Feng Liu is a machine learning researcher with research interests in statistical trustworthy machine learning. Currently, he is the recipient of the ARC DECRA Fellowship, a Senior Lecturer (US Associate Professor) at The University of Melbourne, Australia, and a Visiting Scientist at RIKEN-AIP, Japan. He has served as a Communication Chair of NeurIPS 2026, as a Program Chair of AJCAI 2026, as an Area Chair for AISTATS, ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, as a senior program committee (SPC) member for AAAI, IJCAI. He has received the Australasian AI Emerging Research Award from the Australian Computer Society, the Early Career Researcher Award from the Australian Pattern Recognition Society, the Discovery Early Career Researcher Award from the Australian Research Council, the Outstanding Paper Award from NeurIPS 2022, the Best Paper Award from AAAI 2025 Workshop CoLoRAI, the Best Student Paper Award from FUZZ-IEEE 2019, and the Best Paper Runner-up Award from ECIS 2023."
